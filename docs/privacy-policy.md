# Parichit — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Parichit ("the app") helps you keep your family tree and the people you know in one private place. This policy explains
what information the app handles, where it is stored and the choices you have.

**Contact:** [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] — replace before publishing.

## Summary

- Everything you enter is stored **on your phone**, in an **encrypted** database.
- **Cloud backup is optional.** Only if you sign in is your data copied to our cloud database so you can restore it or
  use it on another phone.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.**
- You can **delete your cloud account and all its data from inside the app** at any time, and erase everything on the
  phone.

## Information you enter

Parichit stores only what you choose to type in:

- **About people** (yourself, relatives and acquaintances): names (in any script), nicknames, gender, dates of birth and
  passing, native village/town, gotra, occupation, organisation, education, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, notes and
  tags.
- **Relationships** between people (parent/child, marriage, friendships and similar connections).
- **Meetings** you record: date, place, people present, notes and follow-up reminders.
- **App settings** such as language, theme and app lock.

Because the app is used to record information about family members and other people you know, please only add
information you are entitled to keep and respect the privacy of the people you add.

The app does **not** read your phone's contacts, call logs, messages, photos or location.

## Where your information is stored

**On your phone.** All data is kept in a database encrypted with a key held in the phone's secure keystore (Android
Keystore). App data is excluded from Android cloud backups. Fingerprint/face unlock is handled entirely by Android; the
app never receives biometric data.

**In the cloud (optional).** If you sign in with your e-mail address, your data is synchronised with a database hosted by
[Supabase](https://supabase.com) (our service provider). Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and protected
by row-level security so that only your account can read or change it. Your e-mail address is used only to sign you in
with a one-time code.

**Files you export.** Backup files are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. CSV and GEDCOM exports are **not**
encrypted; they are created only when you ask and you decide where to save or share them.

## How information is used

Only to provide the app's features: showing your people and relationships, computing how people are related, reminders,
search, backup and sync between your devices. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose,
and we do not share it with third parties except Supabase as the hosting provider for the optional cloud backup.

## Your choices and rights

- **Use the app without an account** — everything works offline on your phone.
- **Delete your cloud account:** More → Cloud backup → *Delete my cloud account*. This permanently deletes your account
  and all data stored in the cloud.
- **Erase the phone:** More → *Erase all data on this phone*.
- **Export your data** at any time: More → Backup & export.
- If you cannot access the app, e-mail [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed in with to request deletion of
  your account and cloud data; we will complete it within 30 days.

## Retention

Data on your phone is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is kept until you delete it or your
account; deleted items may remain in the provider's encrypted backups for a limited period before being purged.

## Children

Parichit is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information as users.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
